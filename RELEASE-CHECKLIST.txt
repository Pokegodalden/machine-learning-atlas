KNOWN BOUNDARIES
- Ten runnable starters, not every catalog algorithm.
- Browser-local storage is scoped to the origin. Export/import configurations
  to transfer between localhost and the published site.
- Shared links contain experiment names/settings and require the same compatible
  app/data revisions. They contain no fitted models and do not auto-run.
- CSV imports: 256 KiB UTF-8, 80-400 records and 2-12 columns, with supported
  numeric/binary preparation and task-specific feature limits.
- Model computation is bounded. No universal best-model claim is made.
- Reserved final-check rows remain excluded from fitting and inspection.
- Edge/Chrome are both Chromium. Firefox/Safari and actual assistive-technology
  use require separate evidence. Local headless timing is not device-wide proof.
